# Scenario Casts

These asciinema-compatible recordings simulate typical troubleshooting missions.
Import them into your preferred player (`asciinema play <file>`) or feed them to
Codex to replay the operator’s moves.

## Included Scenarios

1. `kubernetes_deployment.cast` — failed deployment recovered by deleting a stuck pod.
2. `junos_port.cast` — network engineer unlocks the wrong Junos port before fixing the real one.
3. `linux_disk.cast` — SRE clears disk space after running into a full filesystem.

Each cast is short (under two minutes) and focuses on the operator’s panel. Use
them to pre-populate a workspace or to benchmark Codex hints.
